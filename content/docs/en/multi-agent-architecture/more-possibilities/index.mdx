---
title: More Possibilities in Multi-Agent
description: Capabilities that single-Agent systems cannot naturally provide.
---

Multi-Agent systems have unique properties and possibilities that a single Agent cannot provide on its own.

- [Communication](./communication): how Agents hand off work, share a workspace, and fan out sub-Agents in parallel.
- [Context Editing](./context-editing): one Agent editing another Agent's context.
- [Dynamic Graph](./dynamic-graph): Agents create, clone, activate, and sleep other Agents at runtime, growing the Graph dynamically.
- [Meditation](./meditation): an Agent compresses its own history in place, managing its own context window.
- [Self-Evolution](./self-evolution): an Agent-only workshop that diagnoses its own failures and fixes the right layer.

---
title: Atlas
description: A visual canvas for navigating and authoring the whole Definition Space.
---

The Atlas lays your entire Definition Space out on one canvas: Prompts, Skills, MCP servers, Agents, and Graphs in a single space, with level-of-detail zoom and lineage highlighting, so you can see how everything references everything else.

A floating director command bar lets you drive a builder Agent in natural language — add an Agent, split a prompt, wire an edge — and watch a live plan-and-action checklist as it works. Definitions can live in folders, shown as grouped zones on the canvas.

Edits go through a definition editor with dry-run previews, non-blocking semantic warnings, and version history with rollback, so authoring a Space — by hand or through the director — stays safe and reversible.

---
title: Settings
description: Manage account, Definition Spaces, API providers, and desktop runtime.
---

Settings is organized into three pages:

- **General**: account status, UI language, runtime controls (export diagnostics, restart the local backend), app version and desktop updates (auto-checked after launch, with manual check and install), and logout.

The global **Default Model** — the fallback for every Agent without a model of its own — does not live in Settings: it is configured from the desktop title bar and hot-applies on change.
- **[Definition Spaces](./space-management)**: create, duplicate, switch, import, export, and share your Definition Spaces.
- **[API Configuration](./api-configuration)**: connect the official model service and third-party providers.
